# Global Enterprise AI Governance & Safety Policy
Document Identifier: POL-SEC-2026-V3
Effective Date: January 15, 2026
Department: Global Security & Compliance Office

1. Executive Summary & Purpose
This policy outlines the authorized and prohibited usage of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Large Language Models (LLMs) across all corporate operations, product engineering, and employee workflows. Compliance is mandatory for all contractors, full-time employees, and third-party vendors.

2. Permitted AI Tools and LLM Deployments
2.1 All employees are authorized to use internally hosted, air-gapped LLM deployments approved by the Security Review Board.
2.2 Public cloud-based AI services are strictly prohibited from receiving or processing Tier-1 Restricted Data.
2.3 Any commercial SaaS tool integrating AI capabilities must undergo an Automated Risk Assessment (ARA) prior to procurement.

3. Data Classification and Handling Rules
3.1 Tier-1 Restricted Data:
    - Customer personally identifiable information (PII), including social security numbers, banking details, and biometric records.
    - Source code repositories containing cryptographic signing keys or proprietary algorithmic trading models.
    - Tier-1 data must NEVER be inputted into external cloud LLMs under any circumstances.
3.2 Tier-2 Internal Data:
    - Internal architectural design documents, meeting minutes, and non-sensitive sprint tasks.
    - Tier-2 data may only be processed by enterprise-contracted AI solutions providing zero-data-retention (ZDR) guarantees.

4. Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Verification Requirement
4.1 Any code, architectural blueprint, or legal contract generated or drafted by an LLM must be reviewed and countersigned by a qualified human subject matter expert prior to production deployment.
4.2 Automated decisions impacting employee performance reviews, hiring, or customer creditworthiness cannot be fully delegated to AI models.

5. Incident Reporting and Penalties
5.1 Any accidental data exfiltration or unauthorized injection of sensitive customer data into third-party AI systems must be reported to security-ops@enterprise.internal within 24 hours of discovery.
5.2 Failure to report an AI safety violation within the 24-hour window constitutes gross negligence and may lead to termination of employment and legal action.
